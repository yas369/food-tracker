      🔔 Meal reminders

      Checking…

      Reminders are scheduled on your phone for the next 14 days and keep working with the app closed or after a restart. Each time you open the app, they're topped up. Logging a meal cancels that day's reminder for it.

      Android may delay reminders by several minutes to save battery. To have them arrive on time, allow Alarms & reminders for Plate Check.

      🔔 Get the reminders on your phone

      Most reliable: add them to your calendar. Your phone's calendar alerts you even when this page is closed.

      iPhone: open the file and tap Add All. Android: use the links below. Each adds a daily repeating event.

      Everything is stored only on this phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. Uninstalling, clearing the app's data or changing phones erases it, so export a backup now and then.
